Simple interest: grows linearly
Compound interest> grows exponentially $\rightarrow\ A = P(1+\frac{r}{n})^{nt}$
$A$ is final amount
$P$ is initial investment
$r$ is interest rate as decimal
$n$ is number of compounds per year%
$t$ is time in years

EX: invest $7000 for 5 years that compounds quarterly at an annual interest rate of 6%
$A=7000(1+\frac{0.06}{4})^{4\times5}$
