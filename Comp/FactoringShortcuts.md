

Diff of squares:
$a^2-b^2=(a-b)(a+b)$
EX:
$4 - 81x^2$
2 - 9x
(2-9x)(2+9x)
$x^8-y^8$
$(x^4)^2(y^4)^2$
$(x^4-y^4)(x^4+y^4)$
$((x^2)^2-(y^2)^2)((x^2)^2+(y^2)^2)$

Perfect Squares:
$(a-b)^2$ or $(a+b)^2$
to determine if a binomial is a perfect square check if the first and last terms are perfect squares
EX:
$25x^2+40x+16$
25 is a ps
16 is a ps
$(5x+4)^2$
$5 \times 4 = 20$
$20\times2=40$
40 is the middle term
the rule for checking is:
double the product of a and be is the middle term

sum / diff generic of cubes:
$x^3+y^3=(x+y)(x^2-xy+y^2)$
$x^3-y^3=(x-y)(x^2+xy+y^2)$
EX:
$x^{12}-y^{12}$
$(x^4)^3-(y^4)^3$
$(x^4-y^4)((x^4)^2+x^4y^4+(y^4)^2)$
$(x^4-y^4)(x^8+x^4y^4+y^8)$
$(x^2-y^2)(x^2+y^2)(x^8+x^4y^4+y^8)$
$(x-y)(x+y)(x^2+y^2)(x^8+x^4y^4+y^8)$

EX:
$7x^4-28x^2$
$7x^2(x^2-4)$
