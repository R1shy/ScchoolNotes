Integers: any whole number.
digits: a number that makes up others (0-9)
even numbers: any number divisible without remainder by 2.
positive numbers: any number above 0.
negative numbers: any number below 0.
rational numbers: a number that can be expressed by a ratio of exactly 2 integers.
irrational numbers: a number that cannot be expressed by a ratio of exactly 2 integers.
imaginary numbers: the square root of any negative number.
real numbers: numbers that have points on a numberline.
natural numbers: positive integers (also known as counting numbers).
counting numbers: any positive integer.
transcendental numbers: 2 a number that cannot be represented as the root of an integer.
