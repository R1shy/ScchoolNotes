## Equation
$(\frac{x-h}{r_x})^2\pm(\frac{y-k}{r_y})^2=1$
$(\frac{y-k}{r_y})^2\pm(\frac{x-h}{r_x})^2=1$
Asymptotes equation:
$y-k=\pm\frac{r_y}{r_x}(x-h)$
