## Notes
domain of $log(x)$ is $x\gt0$
$y=log_b^x=b^y=x$
## Log Form
$y=log_{b}^x$
y is the logarithm
x is the argument

## Exponential Form
$y=b^y=x$
y is the exponent

## Example
$log_2^8=3$ or log base 2 of 8 is 3


## Practice
$4^2=16$ or $2=log_4^{16}$
$10^3=1000$ or $3=log_{10}^{1000}$
$y=log_2^{-3}$ or $-3=2^y$ invalid can't log a negative
$y=log_5^0$ or $0=5^y$ invalid can't log $0$

$log_4^x=\frac{1}{2}$ = 2 
$log_\frac{1}{2}^8=x$ = $-3$
