

## Example
$3(\log(x))^2-13\log(x)+4=0$
$u=\log(x)$
$3u^2-13u+4=0$
factor it out
$(3u-1)(u-4)=0$
$u=\frac{1}{3}$
$u=4$


## Example 2 with $e$
$4e^{4x}-5e^{2x}-21=0$
because it ends with 0 exponent and goes down uniformly you can do a factoring trick
$u=e^{2x}$
usually make the middle term $u$
$4u^2-5u-21=0$
factor out
$(4u+7)(u-3)=0$
$u=\frac{-7}{4}$
$u=3$
