## Definition
Diagram of force in a system

##  Notes
Put dot in the center of mass
arrows all face out from the dot
start from the center
***ONLY PUT REAL FORCES***

## Examples of not real forces
Component forces
Net force (do an arrow per source BUT magnitude is the valuer)
centripetal forces (or any force that has another real force causing it)
centrifugal force (or any force that result from non-intertial reference frames)
