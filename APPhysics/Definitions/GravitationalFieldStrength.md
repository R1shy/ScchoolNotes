## Equations
$F_g=mg$
$F_g=\frac{Gm_pm_o}{r^2}$
$g=G\frac{m_p}{r^2}$
$m_1$ is mass of object
$m_2$ is mass of planet
$r$ is the distance between the center of masses of the two objects
G is universal gravity constant $6.67430e-11\frac{Nm^2}{kg^2}$
