## Definition
The rate of change of velocity


## Equations
$a=\frac{\Delta v}{\Delta t}$

$a=\frac{v^2-v^2_0}{2\Delta x}$


## Notes

### Position vs Time graph notes
when the line is flat $v=0$
if the line is becoming less steep the object is slowing down
if the line is becoming more steep the object is speeding up
### Velocity vs Time graph notes
When the line is flat $a=0$
any movement above $v=0$ is considered to be in the positive direction while any below is in the negative
The area under a velocity vs. time curve, regardless of the shape, will equal the object's displacement during that time interval.

### Acceleration vs Time graph notes
The area under an acceleration vs. time curve, regardless of the shape, will equal the object's change in velocity during that time interval.
