## Notes
any force applied to an object that causes it to orbit in a circular path instead of a straight line
it is not a real force but usually caused by either gravity or acceleration

## Centripical Aceceleration
A net force can be applied to change the magnitude of the velocity of an object
it is interesting that a net force can also be applied to the object to change the direction of a velocity
## Equation
$F_{centripical} = \frac{mv^2}{r}$
$a_c=\frac{v^2}{r}$
