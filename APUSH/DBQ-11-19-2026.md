Jefferson and Jackson agreed on some policies and disagreed heavily on others, especially when it involved Native Americans. Jefferson took a strict interpretation of the constitution and believed fiercely in states rights while Jackson believed in a looser interpretation which gave more powers to the federal government. In Document 1 you can see his explicitly as Jefferson rejects the idea of a national bank because there is not support in the constitution for the federal government to do something like this, the context of this is that the federal bank had just come into being and it was created by Jefferson's rival Alexander Hamilton giving him another reason to not like it. In document 6 Jackson agrees with Jefferson on this but for a different reason under him the question of the continuation of the national bank came up and he vetoed continuing it because he thought it would benefit only the rich people of the US and not the reset of the country, He writes this very formally because this is an official veto and his audience is both chambers of congress. In document 3 Jefferson argues that Natives should be protected and even included as farmers in the white farmer communities that are near their lands, The context of this is that he said this during his second inaugural address which set a surprisingly pro-native stance for his second term. In document 5 though Jackson supports the removal of Natives to protect and separate them from white settlers and to allow settlers to push further westward, the context of this is that Jackson was very anti-native this document is an argument for the Indian Removal Act which created the infamous Trail of Tears.
