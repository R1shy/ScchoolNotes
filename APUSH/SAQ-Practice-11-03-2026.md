A similarity between Hamilton's and Clay's plans is that they both wanted to enforce tariffs on foreign exports. For Hamilton he wanted to fund the government through these tariffs, while Clay wanted to protect American manufacturing by forcing citizens to buy from them. Both men wanted to help and boost the economies of their country in the best way at their respective points in time.

Hamilton wanted to Assume state debts to help them while Clay wanted to do general infrastructure improvements in states without assuming their debt. Hamilton wanted to assume debts because it would encourage other countries to want America to succeed now that it owed them money, Clay wanted to improve transportation infrastructure to encourage a sense of national unity and have generalized exports. Both men wanted to support their country in the best way they could at their respective points in time.
