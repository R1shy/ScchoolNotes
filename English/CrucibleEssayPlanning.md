Prompt: In The Crucible, we see people using their freedom of speech to silence others, How does Freedom of Speech become weaponized?
## Intro
can be 4-6 sentences
short and sweet
thesis topic = basis for the hook
what background info does the audience need to know in order to understand this thesis? this is your context

### hook
introduce your subject with a quotation from the dialogue or from an interview with the writer
creatively retell a scene
open with a bold, humorless line

### context
Who, what, where, why
H: optional but historical context
Author
Title (year)

### Thesis
should have a clear argument that answers the prompt and is supported with a consequence ("so what?") or rationale ("because?")


## Intro Paragraph
On January 6th of 2021 Donald Trump abused his free speech to incite an insurrection by his followers against the United States Government. After losing his reelection bid for president he used his rights given to him by the constitution that he swore an oath to protect to dismantle the very government whose laws it is so central to. The actions caused by his abuse directly lead to multiple deaths and a political landscape forever changed.


## Conclusions
The myth: in a conclusion you must restate your thesis and your argument's main points.

Reality: yes, begin by restarting your main argument, but after that, all you're expected to do is provide the paper witha sense of closure.
answer the so what

### Conclusion strategy
1. Full circle: revisit the hook in light of the information present in the essay.
2. Modern day example
3. Personal connection
4. get preachy: connect to a universal theme or consider the larger implications
5. use a quotation
