Prompt: In The Crucible, we see people using their freedom of speech to silence others, How does Freedom of Speech become 
weaponized?

## Intro
can be 4-6 sentences
short and sweet
thesis topic = basis for the hook
what background info does the audience need to know in order to understand this thesis? this is your context

### hook
introduce your subject with a quotation from the dialogue or from an interview with the writer
creatively retell a scene
open with a bold, humorless line

### context
Who, what, where, why
H: optional but historical context
Author
Title (year)

### Thesis
should have a clear argument that answers the prompt and is supported with a consequence ("so what?") or rationale ("because?")




## Conclusions
The myth: in a conclusion you must restate your thesis and your argument's main points.

Reality: yes, begin by restarting your main argument, but after that, all you're expected to do is provide the paper with a sense 
of closure. answer the so what

### Conclusion strategy
1. Full circle: revisit the hook in light of the information present in the essay.
2. Modern day example
3. Personal connection
4. get preachy: connect to a universal theme or consider the larger implications
5. use a quotation
